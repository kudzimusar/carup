CarUp exists because buying a used vehicle asks you to trust a stranger about facts you cannot check. We are building the Automotive Intelligence & Trust Network to change what that decision feels like: every vehicle carries a record of what is actually known about it, and every gap in that record is shown as a gap rather than hidden.
