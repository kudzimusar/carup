Hello Fixture Buyer,

Thank you for joining. I want to tell you why we built CarUp, because it explains what you can expect from us.

Buying or owning a car should not require blind trust. Too often the history is unclear, the paperwork is hard to verify, and the person on the other side of the deal is a stranger. That uncertainty costs people real money.

So we are building CarUp as a trust network, not just a marketplace. Every vehicle can carry evidence. Every claim can be checked. The goal is simple: you should be able to know the car before you commit to it.

Know the car. Trust the journey.
