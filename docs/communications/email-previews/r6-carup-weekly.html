What a service record is actually worth

A stamped book proves someone wrote in a book. A recorded service entry on CarUp ties the work to a garage, a date and a mileage reading — which is the part a buyer, a financier or an insurer can lean on.

If you are selling this year, this is the single cheapest thing you can fix.

Garage tip of the week

Before the rainy season, ask your garage to check door and windscreen seals. It is a ten-minute job that prevents the damp-carpet electrical faults we see every year.
