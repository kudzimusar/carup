# Institutional manual — CarUp Intelligence

For government and regulatory readers.

## The distinction everything here rests on

- **CarUp assessed** — CarUp reviewed documents somebody supplied to it.
- **Registry confirmed** — an authoritative government source said so.

The first exists. **The second does not exist anywhere in CarUp today.** No registry
or revenue-authority provider is registered, and every registry check on record ran
against a sandbox simulator.

Every figure published to you is named for which of the two it is. Nothing CarUp
has reviewed is described as government-verified, officially verified or registry
confirmed, and the assistant is prevented from using those phrases while no
registry has confirmed anything.

## What you can see

| Figure | Basis |
|---|---|
| **Evidence CarUp reviewed** | CarUp's own review of supplied documents |
| **Awaiting review** | Submitted, not yet reviewed |
| **Review decisions** | CarUp's own reviewer decisions, by type |
| **Registry checks** | Split into live confirmations and sandbox simulations, never combined |
| **Audit trail** | Entry **counts** only |

## What you cannot see, and why

- **Any government-verified status.** Nothing has been confirmed by an authoritative
  source.
- **National registration volumes or a national verification backlog.** CarUp is not
  a national vehicle registry and holds no national data. Figures previously shown
  here — a 1.2 million vehicle registry, a pending-verification backlog, a
  verified-today count — were string literals and have been removed.
- **Officer session and MFA audits.** CarUp holds no officer directory and issues no
  officer credentials. A panel listing named officers with IP addresses and
  timestamps was invented and has been removed.
- **Enforcement outcomes.** CarUp records no prosecution, penalty or enforcement
  action.
- **An official duty assessment.** CarUp calculates a duty **estimate** from
  published rates. It is connected to no revenue authority, and the figure is not
  an assessment, a ruling, or an amount anybody owes.

## What you are most likely to misread

**A sandbox match confirms nothing.** It is a simulator agreeing with itself.

**CarUp's review is CarUp's.** A complete evidence review means CarUp examined what
it was given — not that any authority has endorsed it.

**Audit counts are not audit entries.** Entries identify people and are not exposed
to an institutional projection.

**An institutional role is not a platform administrator.** Commercial marketplace
behaviour is not available to institutional roles, and every institutional payload
restates that boundary.
