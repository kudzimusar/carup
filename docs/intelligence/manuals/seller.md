# Seller manual — CarUp Intelligence

For private sellers and vehicle owners.

## What you can see

| Figure | Where it comes from |
|---|---|
| **Listing views** | The activity ledger: one event per listing open, de-duplicated within a session |
| **Unique visitors** | Distinct people over the period, not distinct accounts |
| **Enquiries** | The enquiry record itself, not a behavioural event |
| **Listing completeness** | A fixed rubric of the fields buyers filter on |
| **Lost opportunity** | Filtered searches your listing would have matched but for a missing field |
| **Suggested next steps** | Deterministic rules over the figures above |

## What you cannot see, and why

- **What your vehicle is worth.** CarUp computes no valuation and no valuation
  model is connected. Any figure would be invented.
- **Who looked at your listing.** Aggregate behaviour only. The identity of an
  anonymous visitor is never disclosed to a seller.
- **Your service history or next service date.** CarUp holds no service schedule
  for your vehicle.
- **Your insurance policy, premium or expiry.** CarUp holds no policy record.
- **Mechanics near you, ranked.** CarUp holds no rating or distance index.

Each of these appears on your Gutu AI page as an explicitly unavailable item with
its reason — deliberately, so the questions CarUp cannot answer are visible rather
than quietly missing.

## What you are most likely to misread

**Views are not people.** One person returning three times is three views and one
visitor.

**An enquiry is not a sale**, and not a viewing. It is a first contact.

**Completeness is not quality.** It measures which fields are recorded, nothing
about how good the listing or the vehicle is. It is unrelated to Trust.

**Lost opportunity is not lost money.** It is lost visibility — searches you would
have appeared in.

**Trust is about the vehicle's evidence, not about you.** A thin Trust position
reflects what CarUp can document, not your trustworthiness as a seller.

**"No advice right now" is not always a clean bill of health.** Where a check could
not run because CarUp has not measured what it needs, your next-steps panel says
so explicitly and lists which check and what was missing.
