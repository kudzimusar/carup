# Dealer manual — CarUp Intelligence

For dealerships and their staff.

## What you can see

| Figure | Scope |
|---|---|
| **Your inventory and publication status** | Your organization only |
| **Enquiries received** | Attributed to your organization |
| **Views and unique visitors** | Aggregate behaviour on your listings |
| **Listing completeness across your stock** | Per listing, and in aggregate |
| **Conversion between recorded stages** | Only where the denominator is large enough to be meaningful |

Your scope is your **verified organization membership**, resolved on the server.
It is never taken from anything the browser sends, so it cannot be widened by a
request.

## What you cannot see, and why

- **Platform-wide inventory.** You see your own stock. A platform-wide figure
  presented as dealer inventory was removed in I8.
- **Other dealers' performance.** Comparative benchmarks are published only where a
  cohort is large enough to prevent identifying an individual competitor.
- **Ratings, ROI or campaign performance.** CarUp records no cost against any
  campaign or promotion, so no return can be computed. The dealer promotions table
  is empty.
- **Buyer identities** for anonymous visitors. Aggregate behaviour only.

## What you are most likely to misread

**A conversion rate over a tiny denominator is not a conversion rate.** Where the
sample is too small, CarUp reports insufficient data rather than a percentage that
would swing wildly on one enquiry.

**Your enquiry count is not your market.** It counts enquiries that reached you,
not demand that existed.

**A promotion with no figures is not a promotion that failed.** It is one CarUp has
no record of.

**Trust is per vehicle, not per dealership.** There is no dealer Trust score, and
the "Trust Index" percentages once shown against named organizations were invented
and have been removed.
