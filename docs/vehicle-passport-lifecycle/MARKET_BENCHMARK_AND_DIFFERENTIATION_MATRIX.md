# Vehicle Passport / Trust Lifecycle 1.0 — Market Benchmark and Differentiation Matrix

**Research date:** 2026-08-28
**Purpose:** convert market research into product requirements without copying another product's architecture or visual identity.

## 1. Benchmark thesis

Vehicle-history products usually answer one of four narrower questions:

1. **What happened to this vehicle?**
2. **How risky does its history look?**
3. **What service/maintenance information should the owner remember?**
4. **What official status does a particular authority hold?**

CarUp's opportunity is to combine these questions inside a persistent, governed vehicle lifecycle:

> **What is this vehicle, what is known about it, how do we know it, what can CarUp responsibly conclude, what should the owner/buyer/partner do next, and how does that record survive service, sale and ownership transfer?**

CarUp should benchmark proven capabilities, but its differentiator is not a larger static report. It is a **living automotive operating record** connected to Marketplace, Seller, Verify, Communications, Intelligence, service, parts and ownership.

## 3. Market capabilities CarUp should deliberately adopt

### A. Chronological vehicle history

Required:

- date/time;
- mileage where observed;
- event category;
- source;
- verification/provenance state;
- visibility;
- supporting evidence;
- correction/supersession relationship;
- actor/organization where safe.

Timeline categories should support at least:

- vehicle identity;
- import/customs;
- registration/licensing;
- ownership;
- inspection;
- mileage;
- damage/incident where evidence exists;
- insurance;
- service/maintenance;
- parts;
- listing/publication;
- reservation/transaction;
- ownership transfer.

### B. Mileage integrity

Required:

- multiple dated mileage observations;
- source/provenance per observation;
- monotonicity/rollback analysis;
- conflict handling;
- unknown handling;
- seller-stated mileage distinct from observed/verified mileage;
- visual history chart/timeline where enough data exists.

No “true mileage” claim unless the evidence model supports it.

### C. Damage / incident history

Required capability:

- evidence-backed damage/incident records;
- affected area where known;
- date;
- repair state where known;
- source;
- photos where governed;
- disclosure conflict with Seller statements;
- unknown when no source exists.

No event absence may become “accident free”.

### D. Ownership continuity

Required:

- current ownership/claim relationship;
- prior ownership events;
- transfer pending/completed state;
- privacy-safe history;
- same canonical vehicle through resale/relisting;
- dispute/review path for contested ownership.

### E. Service and maintenance

Required:

- garage/professional service records;
- owner/DIY records;
- mileage at service;
- work performed;
- parts used;
- invoice/evidence;
- source/actor strength;
- recommended next service where supported;
- reminders;
- correction/dispute.

### F. Theft / legal / compliance risk

Required:

- source-specific status;
- adverse state;
- clear/verified state only when positively established;
- no-record distinct from clear;
- unavailable distinct from no-record;
- source not connected distinct from source unavailable.

### G. Market value / pricing context

Potential later Passport capability:

- current listing price;
- historical listing prices;
- price changes;
- comparable market range where model/data quality is sufficient;
- depreciation/value trend only with explicit methodology and uncertainty.

Pricing intelligence is an Intelligence projection, not Vehicle Truth.

### H. Owner care

Required owner-facing direction:

- inspection due;
- licence due;
- insurance due;
- service due;
- unresolved evidence request;
- discrepancy action;
- ownership transfer action;
- PartSentry/part lifecycle attention where supported;
- re-sale readiness.

These actions should route through canonical Communications and be driven by authoritative dates/events where available.

### I. Corrections and disputes

Market history systems can contain errors; CarUp must have a stronger governance model.

Required:

- challenge/correction request;
- evidence attachment;
- review;
- decision;
- supersession rather than silent overwrite;
- audit record;
- re-evaluation of Trust after material governed changes.

## 4. CarUp's differentiation pillars

### 4.1 One vehicle, one evolving Passport

The listing is temporary.

The Passport must persist across:

- pre-sale ownership;
- listing;
- buyer due diligence;
- sale;
- ownership transfer;
- service;
- repairs;
- parts;
- insurance/finance interactions where governed;
- future relisting.

### 4.2 Truth, Trust and Passport remain separate

**Truth:** facts/observations + provenance.

**Trust:** governed evaluation of those facts.

**Passport:** the human and workflow experience around them.

This separation is stronger than a conventional single history score because it lets CarUp explain uncertainty honestly.

### 4.3 Multi-projection Passport

One canonical vehicle should support role-safe projections:

- public;
- buyer/transaction;
- owner;
- seller/dealer;
- garage/mechanic;
- insurer/lender where authorized;
- government/institution where authorized;
- CarUp governance/admin.

The record is shared; permissions and presentation are not.

### 4.4 Zimbabwe-first evidence model

Zimbabwe's vehicle due diligence is fragmented across licensing, registration/ownership, inspection, customs/import and police/theft processes.

CarUp should be the **connective trust record**, not a fake substitute for those institutions.

Unique requirement:

> source connected, document reviewed, pending, rejected, expired, no record, unavailable and unknown must remain semantically distinct.

### 4.5 Marketplace-native Passport

The buyer should not need a separate unrelated report product.

Marketplace should progressively disclose:

**vehicle → commercial decision → Trust summary → evidence/history → Passport**

Seller should contribute once and have the same governed facts project into Marketplace and Passport.

### 4.6 Communications-native lifecycle

The Passport is not passive.

Material lifecycle states can generate canonical CarUp actions/notifications:

- evidence needed;
- discrepancy action;
- verification result;
- service due;
- inspection/licence/insurance due;
- ownership transfer;
- new buyer activity;
- Trust materially changed.

Providers remain transports.

### 4.7 Intelligence-native lifecycle

Every authoritative lifecycle mutation can produce governed observations for:

- owner next-best-action;
- seller listing improvement;
- buyer decision support;
- service demand;
- parts demand;
- partner ROI;
- CarUp Command Centre.

Intelligence may recommend. It may not rewrite Truth or Trust.

### 4.8 Service + PartSentry provenance

This is a major CarUp differentiator.

A future verified chain can be:

**garage job → technician/garage identity → mileage → service performed → part fitted → PartSentry provenance → invoice/photo → Passport event**

This turns post-purchase service into reusable resale evidence.

### 4.9 Ownership-transfer continuity

A vehicle-history report often ends at the purchasing decision.

CarUp's Passport should continue through:

**seller → buyer → transfer → new owner → service → future seller**

This makes CarUp useful across the whole economic life of the vehicle.

### 4.10 Evidence-aware AI instead of AI-as-authority

AI may:

- extract document fields;
- flag inconsistencies;
- summarize history;
- explain Trust;
- suggest next actions;
- identify missing evidence;
- help users understand service patterns.

AI may not:

- approve evidence by itself;
- invent government results;
- certify ownership;
- set canonical Trust directly;
- convert missing data into positive statements.

## 5. Target CarUp experience: “living vehicle operating record”

The Passport should eventually answer, at a glance:

### Identity
**What vehicle is this?**

### Current state
**Who/what relationship is active, is it listed, and what lifecycle state is it in?**

### Trust
**What can CarUp responsibly conclude?**

### Evidence
**Why does CarUp say that?**

### History
**What happened over time?**

### Attention
**What is missing, conflicting, expiring or due?**

### Action
**What should I do next?**

### Continuity
**What remains with the vehicle when the listing or owner changes?**

This is the product target that should guide information architecture and phase certification.

## 6. Benchmark-derived design requirements

The Passport must not look like a database dump.

Required visual patterns:

- vehicle identity hero;
- current Trust state with confidence/evidence basis/limitations;
- chronological timeline;
- mileage trend;
- source/provenance markers;
- discrepancy callouts;
- evidence gallery;
- owner action/attention rail;
- lifecycle status;
- privacy-aware role projections;
- progressive disclosure from summary → event → evidence.

Do not:

- show dozens of green badges;
- use a single score as the whole product;
- hide unknowns;
- make source-unavailable look like pass;
- use stock imagery as evidence;
- make the page dependent on animation;
- crowd mobile with desktop-style dashboards.

## 8. Benchmark safety note

Features and source availability vary by country and provider. The benchmark tells CarUp **what problem users expect solved**, not what CarUp may claim to have verified.

Every implementation phase must prove the actual connected source, evidence route and runtime state before presenting an authoritative claim.
