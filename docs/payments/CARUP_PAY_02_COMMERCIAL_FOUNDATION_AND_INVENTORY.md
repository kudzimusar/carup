## 2. Commercial Category & Financial Authority Model (Tasks 3–6)

### 2.1 Strict Three-Category Separation (`PAY-02-CAT`)
Every commercial or financial event in CarUp is classified into one of three non-overlapping categories:
1. **Category A — First-Party CarUp Commercial Sales (`category_a_first_party`)**:
   - CarUp is the contractual seller, merchant of record, legal beneficiary, and revenue owner (e.g., `intel.report.vehicle_history_single`, `sub.diaspora.buyer_pro`, `imp.sourcing.verification_fee`).
2. **Category B — Third-Party or Multi-Party Commercial Obligations (`category_b_third_party`)**:
   - Dealers, private sellers, independent garages, foreign exporters, shipping lines, clearing agents, ZIMRA customs, insurers, or lenders own the underlying principal or service fee.
   - CarUp **never** recognizes third-party principal, ocean freight, customs duty, or garage repair invoices as CarUp revenue and **never** processes them on CarUp's Japan direct-merchant Stripe account (`acct_1UVnGaEjgXqvlsJk`).
3. **Category C — Corporate Financial Operations & Capital/Treasury (`category_c_corporate_treasury`)**:
   - Accounts payable (cloud hosting, SMS/email providers, PSP fees, payroll), statutory tax remittances, founder equity, shareholder distributions, debt facilities, and joint-venture funding.
   - **Strictly prohibited from customer Checkout** (`checkoutProhibited: true`, `eligibleProviders: []`).

### 2.2 Server-Governed Minor-Unit Pricing & Immutable Quotes (`PAY-02-PRICE`)
- All monetary values in `pay02Contract.js` and `20261031143000_carup_pay02_commercial_foundation.sql` use **integer minor units** (`BIGINT`) with explicit `currencyScale` (`JPY: 0`, `USD: 2`, `ZWG: 2`, `ZAR: 2`, `GBP: 2`, `EUR: 2`).
- Floating-point numbers (`19.99`) are deterministically rejected with `PAY02_FLOAT_OR_UNSAFE_AMOUNT_REFUSED`.
- Client-supplied amounts, currency overrides, merchant overrides, beneficiary overrides, and unapproved FX conversions are deterministically rejected.

### 2.4 Mobile Channel & App-Store Compliance Policy (Section 6)
Every catalogue entry enforces an explicit `mobileChannelPolicy`:
- `digital_web_only_until_iap`: Digitally consumed in-app products (reports, digital certificates, digital subscriptions, ad credits) must **not** trigger external Stripe/Paynow web checkout inside native iOS/Android webviews where Apple App Store / Google Play in-app purchase rules apply.
- `physical_service_exempt`: Real-world physical vehicle purchases, mechanical garage repairs, ocean freight, and towing are physical-world services exempt from digital IAP rules, but remain gated by their respective provider authorities.
- `b2b_contract_exempt`: Contracted B2B enterprise/fleet obligations.
- `internal_treasury_prohibited`: Category C internal corporate/treasury operations, never exposed on mobile or web checkout.

## 4. First Real CarUp Product Selection & Pilot Readiness Contract (Task 9 — `PAY-02-PILOT`)

### 4.1 Selected Pilot Product: `intel.report.vehicle_history_single`
- **Title**: CarUp Canonical Vehicle History & Provenance Report (Single VIN)
- **Why Selected**:
  1. **100% First-Party CarUp Ownership (Category A)**: CarUp is the contractual seller, merchant of record (`carup_japan`), funds owner, and revenue beneficiary. Zero third-party escrow, customs, or seller payout dependencies exist.
  2. **Real Fulfillment Engine Already Exists in Source**: Unlike the synthetic `PAY-01C` demo, `backend/services/report/reportService.js` already implements `assembleReport(db, vin, { audience: 'buyer' })` and `generateReportVersion(db, vin, { actorId, audience })` backed by `public.vehicle_report_versions`, `public.vehicles`, and `public.vehicle_evidence`.
  3. **Compatible with Accepted `PAY-01A/B/C` Stripe Japan Sandbox**: Uses `stripe_carup_japan_sandbox` (`acct_1UVnGaEjgXqvlsJk`) in JPY minor units with canonical `public.payment_transactions` convergence.
- **Implemented Readiness Contract in PAY-02**:
  - `deliverVerifiedVehicleHistoryReport` in `backend/services/payment/pay02Service.js` verifies:
    - The buyer's authenticated identity matches both the `pay02_commercial_quotes` record and `payment_transactions.pay01a_actor_id`.
    - The quote is for `intel.report.vehicle_history_single` and binds the exact target VIN (`sourceObjectId`).
    - The canonical `payment_transactions` row is verified `status = 'paid'` with both `pay01a_checkout_id` and `pay01a_intent_id` populated.
    - The buyer's entitlement order is active (`grantedAt !== null && revokedAt === null`).
    - Only when all checks pass does it invoke `assembleReport` and `generateReportVersion` to deliver the real versioned report.

### 4.2 Exact Downstream Work Order: `PAY-03A`
- **Unit ID**: `PAY-03A — End-to-End Sandbox Checkout & Real Vehicle History Report Fulfillment`
- **Scope**:
  1. Bind buyer VIN report checkout initiation to `pay02_issue_commercial_quote` + `pay02_claim_quote_checkout` + `PAY-01A` Stripe Japan sandbox Checkout session creation.
  2. Upon signed webhook / provider reconciliation (`pay02_sync_quote_from_canonical_payment`), automatically issue the VIN-scoped entitlement and generate the immutable `vehicle_report_versions` snapshot via `deliverVerifiedVehicleHistoryReport`.
  3. Upon admin refund via `PAY-01B`, automatically revoke the VIN report entitlement and invalidate active share tokens in `vehicle_report_shares`.
